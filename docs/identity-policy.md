# Identity policy

An entity is one physical establishment at one location. Nearby branches, shared domains, addresses and phones do not establish identity. Relocation, ownership changes and ambiguous renaming require review. A missing counterpart is valid. Missing records do not prove closure.

SAME requires evidence of the same physical establishment. DIFFERENT requires evidence of distinct establishments. UNCERTAIN preserves ambiguity. Cannot-link constraints apply across the entire proposed component. A–B and B–C never override an A–C prohibition. Every review binds immutable record versions. Changes invalidate pending proposals; accepted history remains auditable. Undo adds an event and restores prior membership without deleting history.
